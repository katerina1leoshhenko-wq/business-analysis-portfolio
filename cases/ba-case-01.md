# BA Case 01: Paper-Based Document Handover to CDE Workflow

## Context

The case examines a document handover process in road infrastructure construction. The original workflow relied on paper documents, physical transfer, manual signatures, and paper-based logs.

## Problem

The AS-IS process created delays and control risks:

- unclear current document version;
- strictly sequential approvals;
- physical delivery as a process bottleneck;
- manual handover records without a reliable audit trail;
- repeated comment cycles without a clear escalation mechanism.

## Analyst Contribution

- Interviewed stakeholders and reconstructed the AS-IS process.
- Identified bottlenecks and root causes.
- Designed a TO-BE process based on CDE, electronic signatures, status tracking, and version control.
- Prepared BPMN 2.0 diagrams and case documentation in English and Russian.

## Outcome

The TO-BE model shows how the process can move from manual document logistics to traceable digital workflow while preserving business responsibilities and auditable handover points.
