# BA Case 02: Regulatory Text to BPMN 2.0 Process Model

## Context

Many infrastructure processes are described in regulations, standards, tables, annexes, and semi-formal diagrams. These sources are readable for domain experts but not directly usable by information systems.

## Problem

Regulatory text often mixes roles, activities, conditions, documents, and control points in prose. To turn it into a process model, a business analyst must separate the process logic, validate ambiguities, and add missing BPMN semantics.

## Analyst Contribution

- Extracted roles, activities, documents, messages, conditions, and outputs from regulatory material.
- Used AI-assisted analysis as a drafting aid, then validated and corrected the structure manually.
- Built AS-IS and TO-BE BPMN 2.0 models.
- Decomposed the process into supporting UML/PlantUML views.
- Documented gaps between regulatory prose and formally valid BPMN models.

## Outcome

The case demonstrates a repeatable approach for transforming regulatory process descriptions into structured process models suitable for discussion, validation, and later digital implementation.
